Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start using SiloVPN?

Simply download the app, open it, and tap the central shield button. The app will automatically configure the best server using the WireGuard® protocol for elite performance.

Is my data really private?

Yes. We operate under a strict no-logs policy. Your data is encrypted at the device level, meaning your browsing activity is invisible even to us.

I'm experiencing slow speeds. What should I do?

Try switching to a different server location or restarting the app. Ensure you have a stable local internet connection for the best results.

Can I use SiloVPN on multiple devices?

SiloVPN is currently optimized for Android. We are working on expanding to other platforms very soon. Stay tuned for updates!
