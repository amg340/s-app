Privacy Policy

At SiloVPN, we believe that privacy is a fundamental human right. Our application is designed to be lightweight, secure, and transparent.

1. Introduction & Commitment

This Privacy Policy explains how we process information and, more importantly, what we do NOT collect. By using SiloVPN, you consent to the practices described herein.

2. Core Functionality: Android VpnService

To provide its core functionality, SiloVPN utilizes the Android VpnService API to create a secure, encrypted tunnel at the system level for your traffic.

  • Implementation: Encapsulation and protection from third-party interception.
  • Protocol: Built on the WireGuard® protocol for elite-grade security.

3. Strict No-Logs Guarantee

We operate under a rigorous "No-Logs" policy. We do NOT collect, store, or share:

  • Online Activity (Websites, Apps, Content).
  • IP Addresses & Connection Identifiers.
  • DNS Records & Session Metadata.

4. Technical Diagnostics

To maintain stability, we collect minimal, anonymized data (Device model, OS version, crash reports). This data is strictly non-identifiable and never sold.
