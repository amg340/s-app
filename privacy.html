Privacy Policy

At SiloVPN, we believe that privacy is a fundamental human right. Our application is designed to be lightweight, secure, and transparent.

1. Introduction & Commitment

This Privacy Policy explains how we process information and, more importantly, what we do NOT collect. By using SiloVPN, you consent to the practices described herein.

2. Core Functionality: Android VpnService

To provide its core functionality, SiloVPN utilizes the Android VpnService API to create a secure, encrypted tunnel at the system level for your outgoing and incoming internet traffic.

  • Implementation: This tunnel ensures that all data packets are encapsulated and protected from third-party interception.
  • Protocol: Our infrastructure is built on the WireGuard® protocol, providing high-speed performance and maximum security.

3. Strict No-Logs Guarantee

We operate under a rigorous "No-Logs" policy. We do NOT collect, store, or share:

  • Online Activity: No visibility into websites visited or apps used.
  • Connection Identifiers: No logging of original IP, assigned IP, or timestamps.
  • DNS Records: Your queries remain private and unrecorded.
  • Session Metadata: No tracking of usage patterns.

4. Limited Technical Diagnostics

To maintain stability, we collect minimal, anonymized diagnostic data (Device model, OS version, crash reports). This data is strictly non-identifiable and used only for internal enhancements.

5. Global Compliance

We comply with international standards (GDPR, CCPA). SiloVPN does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. While our Terms require users to be 18+, we protect all users regardless of age.

6. User Rights

You have the right to request information about the technical data we process or request the deletion of anonymized diagnostic logs associated with your device.

7. Security Measures

We implement industry-standard encryption and secure protocols to protect user data. Our servers are optimized for lightning-fast speeds without compromising security.
