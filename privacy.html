Privacy Policy for SiloVPN

At SiloVPN, we believe that privacy is a fundamental human right. Our application is designed to be lightweight, secure, and transparent. This Privacy Policy explains how we process information and, more importantly, what we do NOT collect. By using SiloVPN, you consent to the practices described herein.

1. Introduction & Commitment

At SiloVPN, we believe that privacy is a fundamental human right. Our application is designed to be lightweight, secure, and transparent. This Privacy Policy explains how we process information and, more importantly, what we do NOT collect. By using SiloVPN, you consent to the practices described herein.

2. Core Functionality: Android VpnService

To provide its core functionality, SiloVPN utilizes the Android VpnService API to create a secure, encrypted tunnel at the system level for your outgoing and incoming internet traffic.

  • Implementation: This tunnel ensures that all data packets are encapsulated and protected from third-party interception.
  • Protocol: Our infrastructure is built on the WireGuard® protocol, which provides high-speed performance and utilizes state-of-the-art cryptographic primitives to ensure maximum security.

3. Strict No-Logs Guarantee

We operate under a rigorous "No-Logs" policy. We do NOT collect, store, or share:

  • Online Activity: We have no visibility into the websites you visit, apps you use, or content you access.
  • Connection Identifiers: We do not log your original IP address, assigned VPN IP address, or connection timestamps.
  • DNS Records: All DNS queries remain private and are not recorded by our system.
  • Session Metadata: We do not track session durations or data usage patterns.

4. Limited Technical Diagnostics

To maintain service stability and fix bugs, we may collect minimal, anonymized diagnostic data:

  • Device Metadata: Device model and Operating System version.
  • Performance Logs: Aggregated crash reports and connection success rates.
  • Privacy Assurance: This data is strictly non-identifiable, anonymized, and used only for internal technical enhancements. We do NOT sell or trade this data with any third parties.

5. Global Compliance & Children’s Privacy

We comply with international data protection standards (including GDPR, CCPA, and Google Play’s Families Policy):

  • Minor Protection: SiloVPN does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13, or the legal minimum age required by local jurisdiction.
  • Legal Consistency: While our Terms of Service require users to be at least 18 years old, we implement these safety measures to protect all users regardless of age.

6. User Rights (Data Subject Rights)

Under global privacy laws, you have the right to:

  • Request information about the minimal technical data we process.
  • Request the deletion of any anonymized diagnostic logs associated with your device.
  • Object to any data processing activities for technical diagnostics.

7. Security Measures

We implement industry-standard encryption and secure protocols to protect user data and ensure all communications remain safe and private. Our servers are optimized for lightning-fast speeds and low latency without compromising security.
