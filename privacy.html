Privacy Policy

At SiloVPN, we believe that privacy is a fundamental human right. Our application is designed to be lightweight, secure, and transparent.

1. Introduction & Commitment

This Privacy Policy explains how we process information and, more importantly, what we do NOT collect. By using SiloVPN, you consent to the practices described herein.

2. Core Functionality: Android VpnService

To provide its core functionality, SiloVPN utilizes the Android VpnService API to create a secure, encrypted tunnel at the system level for your internet traffic.

  • Implementation: This tunnel ensures all data packets are encapsulated and protected from third-party interception.
  • Protocol: Powered by the WireGuard® protocol, providing high-speed performance and state-of-the-art cryptographic primitives.

3. Strict No-Logs Guarantee

Rigorous No-Logs Policy

We do NOT collect, store, or share your data. Our infrastructure is built to be blind to your activity:

  • Online Activity: No visibility into websites, apps, or content.
  • Connection Identifiers: No logging of original IP, assigned IP, or timestamps.
  • DNS Records: All DNS queries remain private and unrecorded.
  • Session Metadata: No tracking of session durations or usage patterns.

4. Limited Technical Diagnostics

To maintain stability, we may collect minimal, 100% anonymized diagnostic data:

  • Device metadata (Model and OS version).
  • Aggregated crash reports and connection success rates.

This data is non-identifiable and used only for internal technical enhancements. We do NOT sell or trade this data.

5. Global Compliance & Children’s Privacy

We comply with international standards (GDPR, CCPA, and Google Play’s Families Policy):

  • Minor Protection: We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
  • Safety Measures: While users must be 18+, we implement safety protocols for all users.

6. User Rights

Under global laws, you have the right to request information about processed technical data, request deletion of diagnostic logs, or object to data processing activities.

7. Security Measures

We implement industry-standard encryption and secure protocols to ensure all communications remain safe and private. Our servers are optimized for lightning-fast speeds without compromising security.
